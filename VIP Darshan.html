📅 How It Works: From Arrival to Darshan

Your complete journey guide from arrival in India to darshan completion

1

Travel & Arrival

Upon landing in India, enter Tirupati by road or air. For visa-stamped entry, make sure your entry date is within the last three months to qualify for NRI darshan.

Entry stamp must be within last 3 months
2

Ticket Booking & Priority Entry

Reserve the NRI darshan slot online via TTD or through us. Entry is via Supadam gate (usually open from 12 PM to 6 PM, subject to seasonal changes).

Supadam gate: 12 PM to 6 PM
3

Srivani Trust VIP Darshan Option

₹10,000 donation + ₹500 VIP ticket

  • Daily quota: 200 tickets available at Tirupati airport, 800 at Tirumala's Srivani counters
  • Offers a 4:30 PM same‑day darshan slot from August 1, 2025 (trial phase)
  • Pre‑booked VIP tickets for August via online channels retain 10 AM slots until October
4

Darshan Day

Arrive at the Supadam gate at your allotted time with your passport and entry stamp. We assist with access and queue guidance to ensure smooth entry.

Professional assistance throughout the process

📝 Tips & Updates (2025)

Latest updates and important information for your darshan planning

Same-Day Darshan Facility

From August 1, TTD started a same-day darshan facility for Srivani donors. Slots shift to 4:30 PM as part of trial operations.

New Srivani Ticket Centre

A new Srivani ticket centre opened in Tirumala in July 2025 to reduce crowding and expedite donor ticket distribution.

Advance Booking

Expect online booking slots to become available approximately 60 days in advance; early reservations are recommended.
