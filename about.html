About Tirupati Taxiwala

Your trusted travel partner for spiritual journeys, temple tours, and city rides in Tirupati and beyond.

Our Story

Founded with a vision to make travel in Tirupati seamless and spiritual, TirupatiTaxiWala has been serving pilgrims, tourists, and locals for over 15 years. Our journey began with a single cab and a commitment to reliability, comfort, and customer satisfaction. Today, we are proud to be the most trusted taxi service in Tirupati, offering a modern fleet and personalized service for every traveler.

Why Choose Us?

  • 24x7 AC cabs for all your travel needs
  • Experienced, courteous, and local drivers
  • Transparent pricing and easy booking
  • Special temple tour packages and darshan assistance
  • Clean, sanitized, and well-maintained vehicles

Our Mission

To provide safe, comfortable, and reliable transportation for every spiritual journey and city ride in Tirupati, ensuring every customer feels valued and cared for.

Our Vision

To be the leading taxi service in South India, known for our integrity, customer focus, and commitment to excellence in every ride.
